IDS Shuishu (tentative code points of the proposed Shuishu block)

The glyph outlines are the Shuishu characters as the ISO/IEC 10646 code charts print them: they are
taken from the font `Uni1B300Shuishu` embedded in the FPDAM 2.2 chart document by its editor, and
reached here through the document's own ToUnicode map. The chart document is
<https://www.unicode.org/wg2/docs/n4922-5theditionPdam2-2-charts.pdf>, SHA-256
b200ce0f5ec06bb97a6c16f08507ba531de88b2f88395462a4915be5824ddd6e, published by the Unicode
Consortium for ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 2/WG 2. The characters come from WG2 N4758 and its revisions.

The code points are the tentative allocations of that amendment: U+1B300..U+1B4E5 for the proposed
logograms and U+1B500..U+1B52E for its 47 radicals. Unicode 18.0.0 leaves them unassigned, so text
in this font is not claimed to be assigned Unicode, as with the bundled Small Seal and New Korean
allocations. The font carries the chart glyphs only; it is a rendering aid for the editor.

Neither the chart document nor its embedded font states separate glyph redistribution terms. The
outlines are reproduced unmodified as a rendering aid and credited to their sources.

The names, the N4758 readings and the meanings in the accompanying data are from Andrew West's
`Shuishu.xlsx`, <https://www.babelstone.co.uk/Shuishu/Shuishu.xlsx>, which indexes the proposed
characters for his Shuishu fonts. That file states no licence either; the readings and meanings are
facts and are credited.

Rebuild the font and the outline records with `uv run scripts/build_shuishu.py`. `data/shuishu/`
holds the pinned sources and their digests.
